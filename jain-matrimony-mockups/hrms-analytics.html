How to read this score
This is a comparison score, not a pass or fail grade. Stronger outcomes earn more credit than routine actions.
  • Handling a profile adds 1 point.
  • Giving a recommendation adds 2 points.
  • Completing a follow-up adds 3 points.
  • Finishing a meeting adds 4 points.
  • Converting a meeting adds 6 points.
  • Closing a task adds 3 points.
  • Completing a match adds 8 points.
The user with the highest total points becomes 100%; everyone else is shown relative to that top user.
